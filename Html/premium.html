Frequently asked questions

How does Spotify Premium work?

Premium gives you access to features such as ad-free music listening, offline downloads and playing songs in any order.

Can I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Spotify account settings, subject to the terms of your plan.

What is the difference between the plans?

Individual is for one person, Student is for eligible verified students, Duo is for two people living together, and Family is for eligible members living together.

Can I listen to music offline?

Premium lets you download music to supported devices so you can listen offline.

Where can I use Spotify?

You can listen on supported phones, computers, tablets, smartwatches, speakers and other devices.
